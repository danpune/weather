    📲 Put Family Weather on your home screen

    Then it opens like an app, with one tap.

    iPhone or iPad

    1. Open this page in Safari.
    2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up). On newer iPhones it is inside the ⋯ menu.
    3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

    Android

    1. Open this page in Chrome.
    2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
    3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Add.

    Mac

    1. In Safari: File menu → Add to Dock.
    2. In Chrome: ⋮ menu → Cast, save and share → Install page as app.

    Opened it from WhatsApp? First tap ⋯ and choose Open in Safari or Open in Chrome.
